# data/

Download the videos [TBD: link] and place them **exactly** here:

```
data/videos/easy_01.mp4 ... hard_02.mp4
data/videos/videos.json       {"easy_01": {"num_frames": F, "fps": 30, "width": W, "height": H}, ...}
data/videos/ATTRIBUTION.txt   sources and licenses of the clips
```

Frame `t` is the `t`-th frame (0-based) of `<video_id>.mp4`. All videos show a right hand. No camera
intrinsics are given. The contents of `data/` are not committed; do not redistribute the clips outside
this course.
