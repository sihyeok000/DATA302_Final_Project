# Source and license of the example

`examples/` is made from one recording of the [HO-Cap](https://irvlutd.github.io/HOCap/) dataset
(not one of the course videos), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

> Wang et al. *HO-Cap: A Capture System and Dataset for 3D Reconstruction and Pose Tracking of
> Hand-Object Interaction.* NeurIPS 2025 Datasets and Benchmarks Track.

Changes by the course TAs: the frames were cut and re-saved as JPEG; the result files were made
from the dataset with errors added on purpose (they are not ground truth).
